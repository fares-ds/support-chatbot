# Roaming and international calls

Roaming lets you use your Acme Mobile line abroad. On a monthly plan, roaming is on unless you turn it off in the Acme app. On prepaid, turn it on before you leave by dialling `*500#`, and make sure you have enough credit.

## Roaming zones

Prices depend on the country you are in:

- **Zone 1**: Tunisia, Morocco, France, Spain, Italy and Turkey.
- **Zone 2**: the other countries of the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar and Egypt.
- **Zone 3**: every other country.

On a ship or a plane, satellite networks are charged at zone 3 prices whatever the country.

## Roaming passes

A pass gives you data and minutes abroad for a fixed price. Buy it in the Acme app, under Travel, before or during your trip. A pass starts when you first use your phone in the country, not when you buy it.

| Pass | Zone 1 | Zone 2 | Zone 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day: 1 GB and 30 minutes | 500 DA | 800 DA | 1,500 DA |
| Week: 5 GB and 100 minutes | 2,000 DA | 3,200 DA | 6,000 DA |

Pro includes a 5 GB pass for zone 1 every month. Without a pass, data abroad costs 300 DA per 100 MB, calls cost 80 DA a minute in zone 1 and 150 DA a minute elsewhere, and receiving a call costs half the price of making one.

## Calling other countries from Algeria

International calls from Algeria cost 40 DA a minute to zone 1, 70 DA to zone 2 and 120 DA to zone 3. Plus includes 30 minutes a month to zone 1, and Pro includes 120 minutes. Unused international minutes do not carry over.

## No surprises abroad

We send you an SMS when you arrive in a country, with its prices. We send another when you have used 80% and then all of a pass. Spending on data abroad outside a pass stops at 5,000 DA a month, unless you raise or remove that limit in the Acme app, under Travel, then Limits. If your phone shows no network abroad, restart it and choose a partner network by hand in its network settings; our partner networks are listed in the app for each country.

## Using data abroad wisely

Phones use data in the background for updates, backups and photo uploads. Before you travel, turn off automatic updates and backups over mobile data, and download maps and playlists while you are on Wi-Fi. The Acme app shows how much of your pass you have used, and you can buy another pass at any time, even before the first one runs out. Data you do not use in a pass is lost when the pass ends.

## Receiving calls and texts abroad

Receiving texts abroad is free everywhere. Receiving a call costs half the price of making one, and is free with a pass. Voicemail works abroad as at home, but calling your voicemail costs the price of a call. To avoid charges for calls you miss, set your phone to send unanswered calls to voicemail after 15 seconds, or turn voicemail off while you travel.

## Coming home

When you come back to Algeria, your phone switches to our network by itself. If you still see a foreign network after you land, restart your phone. Passes you did not use stay in your account for 12 months and can be used on your next trip.
