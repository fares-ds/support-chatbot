# Plans and prices

Acme Mobile has three monthly plans. Every plan includes unlimited calls and texts to Acme Mobile numbers, voicemail, and the Acme app. Prices include tax, and you pay at the start of each billing month.

## The three plans

**Basic** costs 1,000 DA a month. It includes 10 GB of mobile data, 300 minutes of calls to other networks, and 100 texts to other networks. Basic suits customers who mostly use Wi-Fi and call within Acme Mobile.

**Plus** costs 2,000 DA a month. It includes 40 GB of mobile data, unlimited calls and texts to every network in Algeria, and 30 minutes of international calls to the zone 1 countries listed on our roaming page. Data you do not use carries over to the next month, up to 20 GB.

**Pro** costs 3,500 DA a month. It includes unlimited mobile data at full speed up to 200 GB, then at 1 Mbit/s until the end of the month. It also includes unlimited calls and texts to every network in Algeria, 120 minutes of international calls to zone 1, and a roaming pass of 5 GB a month in zone 1. Pro includes a second SIM card for a tablet or a watch, which shares the plan's data.

## Changing your plan

You can change your plan at any time in the Acme app, under Account, then Plan, or by asking us in the chat.

- **Moving to a bigger plan** takes effect at once. You pay the difference for the days left in the billing month, and the new allowances start straight away. Data you used before the change counts against the new allowance.
- **Moving to a smaller plan** takes effect at the start of your next billing month, so you keep what you paid for until then. You can cancel a pending move until the day before it takes effect.
- You can change your plan once per billing month. Moving back to the plan you had before counts as a change.

Changing plans never changes your number, your SIM card or your contract's end date.

## Family plans

A family plan joins up to five lines on one bill. Each line keeps its own plan, and every line after the first gets 15% off its monthly price. The account owner pays the bill and can see each line's usage, but not its calls or messages. To create a family plan, the account owner adds lines in the Acme app, under Account, then Family. Each person added confirms with a code sent to their phone.

## When you run out of data

When your data allowance runs out, you can keep browsing at 128 kbit/s until your next billing month, or buy a data top-up in the Acme app: 5 GB for 400 DA or 15 GB for 1,000 DA. Top-ups last until the end of the billing month. Calls and texts are not affected.

## Extras and their prices

Some services cost extra on every plan:

- **International calls** beyond your plan's minutes are charged at the rates on our roaming page.
- **Premium numbers**, such as game lines and paid information services, cost what the service charges, plus 5 DA a minute. You can block them for free in the Acme app, under Account, then Restrictions.
- **A second number on the same SIM card** costs 200 DA a month. It is useful for keeping work and personal calls apart.
- **Paper bills** cost 50 DA each. Bills by email and in the app are free.

## Fair use

Unlimited calls and texts are for personal use. We may ask you to move to a business plan if a line makes more than 3,000 minutes of calls or sends more than 3,000 texts in a month, or is used for automatic calling, call centres or reselling minutes. Unlimited data on Pro is for use on the phone and the shared SIM card; sharing it with other devices through your phone's hotspot is limited to 50 GB a month.

## Moving from prepaid to a monthly plan

You can move your prepaid line to a monthly plan without changing your number. Choose the plan in the Acme app, under Account, then Plan, and confirm with your ID. Your remaining prepaid credit is added to your first bill as a discount, up to 2,000 DA, and any active data option keeps running until it ends.
