# Bills and payments

Customers on a monthly plan get one bill a month. Your billing month starts on the day of the month you joined Acme Mobile: if you joined on the 12th, each billing month runs from the 12th to the 11th of the next month.

## Your monthly bill

Your bill is ready on the first day of each billing month. It shows the plan's price for the month ahead, anything you used beyond your plan during the month that ended, such as international calls, roaming or data top-ups, and any discounts. We send it by email as a PDF, and you can see your last 24 bills in the Acme app, under Account, then Bills.

Your first bill can be higher than the next ones, because it covers the days between joining and the start of your first full billing month as well as the month ahead.

## Paying your bill

You have 15 days after the bill is ready to pay it. You can pay:

- **In the Acme app**, with a CIB or Edahabia card. The payment shows on your account at once.
- **By direct debit** from a bank account. We take the amount on the due date, and you can set it up in the app, under Account, then Payment.
- **In cash** at any Acme Mobile shop. Bring your phone number or your bill.

If you pay by direct debit and the payment fails, we tell you by SMS and you have 5 days to pay another way before the late payment steps below begin.

## Late payment

If your bill is still unpaid 10 days after the due date, calls and texts you make are blocked, but you can still receive calls and call emergency numbers. After 30 days unpaid, the line is suspended. Restoring a suspended line costs 200 DA, added to your next bill, and the line works again within an hour of payment.

If you cannot pay on time, ask us in the chat before the due date. We can split a bill into two or three payments once in any 12 months.

## Questioning a charge

If a charge on your bill looks wrong, tell us within 60 days of the bill date, in the chat or at a shop. We check the charge within 5 working days. While we check it, you only need to pay the rest of the bill. If the charge was wrong, we take it off your next bill, or refund it to the card or bank account you paid with within 10 working days.

## Refunds and credits

If you pay more than you owe, the difference stays on your account as credit and comes off your next bill. You can ask for a refund instead: it goes back to the card or bank account you paid with, within 10 working days. Credit on an account you close is refunded automatically.

## Bills on a family plan

On a family plan, the account owner gets one bill for every line. The bill shows each line's monthly price, its discount and its extra charges on a separate page. Every line's user can see their own usage in the app, but only the owner can see the whole bill, pay it, and change how it is paid. If a family bill is unpaid, the late payment steps apply to every line on it at once.

## Receipts and tax

Every payment you make gets a receipt, which you can download from the app, under Account, then Payments. Companies can add their tax number and address to their account, under Account, then Profile, so that their bills show them. A bill that has already been issued cannot be changed, but we can send you a corrected copy within 3 working days if you ask in the chat.

## Closing your account with an unpaid bill

If you close a line while a bill is unpaid, you still owe the bill, and we send a final bill for anything used since. Unpaid bills of a closed line block you from opening a new line until they are paid.
